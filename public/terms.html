The service

BlueBar is a point-of-sale and management app for bars and restaurants. The BlueBar connection for ChatGPT and Claude lets a business's manager ask questions about that business's own data. It is read-only.

Your account and your data

  • You are responsible for who can sign in to your business as a manager, and for keeping PINs and sign-in details private.
  • Only connect an assistant account that you control. Anyone who can use that assistant account can read the data the connection allows. Disconnect it if that changes.
  • Your business data stays yours. See the Privacy policy for what is stored and shared.

Answers from an assistant

Figures come from your BlueBar records, but the assistant writes the answer. Check important numbers in BlueBar's reports before relying on them for accounting, tax or payroll. BlueBar's own records remain the source of truth.

Availability

We work to keep BlueBar available but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. We may change or end the connection, for example to follow the assistant providers' rules.
