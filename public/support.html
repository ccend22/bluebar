BlueBar

BlueBar for ChatGPT · Support

Connect

  1. In ChatGPT, open BlueBar and choose Connect.
  2. On the BlueBar page, enter your business code. If asked, sign in to BlueBar as a manager in the tab that opens, then come back.
  3. Approve access. Approve sales access too if you want answers about sales, payments and waiters.
  4. Choose Vazhdo te ChatGPT and ask, for example, "Sa shitje kam sot?"

Disconnect

Sign in to BlueBar as a manager and open Cilësimet → Integrimet → Menaxho lidhjet, then disconnect. Access stops immediately.

Common problems

  • "A BlueBar manager must approve read access to sales": connect again and approve sales access.
  • The connection stopped working: connections end after 30 days, or when a manager disconnects or is deactivated. Connect again.
  • Only managers can connect. Waiter accounts cannot.
  • Dates use Albanian time (Europe/Tirane).
